Entry for the [One Word From Grandma bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-word-from-grandma-f04bed16) on make.fun: exactly one word — the word grandma would text about AI — written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The entry

> robit

5 characters.

## Why it should win

It is the typo a grandma would actually make, warm and a little baffled in five letters: she means robot, she typed it the way she heard it, and she is not embarrassed about it at all. The joke lands the moment you read it, with no caption and no context needed — a grandmother confidently misspelling the machine she is slightly afraid of and slightly fond of at the same time. It is tied to AI and robots by its very misspelling, it sounds exactly like her texting voice, and it is fresh: not the film reference a grandson would pick, not the obvious correct word, but the word she would really send.

`main.py` scores a shortlist of twelve original words against the judge's brief, in the judge's own order — grandma's word, her voice, funny in one word, tied to AI, fresh — and writes the highest-scoring word deterministically.
